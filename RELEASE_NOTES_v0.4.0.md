## What's new

**New visual identity: "Adire Ledger"**
- Dyed-indigo surfaces with a procedural adire (resist-dye) motif, live-indigo actions, and turmeric reserved for "what's next".
- Home: deposited total counts up in a hero panel, withdrawn shown as a share bar, tappable Beels/Transactions counts, and a new **Coming up** section with your next contributions on date tiles.
- Beel detail: header is now a **rota ring**, one arc per contributor filling as they pay, with paid count and collected amount.
- Login, register and forgot-password share a branded indigo banner.
- Beels list: indigo icon tiles with name and amount alongside; larger display app-bar titles; indigo new-beel button.
- Pages fade in with a short rise (disabled under reduced motion).

## Notes
- Dark mode is not included yet.
- Transactions, Groups and Profile pick up the new palette but have no signature layouts yet.
